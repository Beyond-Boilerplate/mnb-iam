## Learning objectives

You will be able to:

- Deliver a 20–30 minute live demo of agentic IAM workflows on Meridian IIQ
- Teach skills, agents, hooks, and MCPs with concrete friction reductions
- Retro your 30 days into a personal mastery scorecard
- Choose next-quarter automations without boiling the ocean

## IAM pain (today’s tax)

If you can’t demo it, the org won’t adopt it — and your friction returns. Capstone is change management.

## Claude concept

**Teach-back test:** explain each primitive with one Meridian example and one failed anti-pattern.

Demo storyboard (suggested):

1. Hooks block a `base/ga` foot-gun (60s)  
2. Lab bootstrap skill → PASS table (2 min)  
3. Jira/fixture → JML or agg triage plan (5 min)  
4. Parallel XML/BeanShell review (5 min)  
5. Verify MCP/bridge → evidence folder (3 min)  
6. CAB pack excerpt (3 min)  
7. Q&A / limitations honestly (remainder)
